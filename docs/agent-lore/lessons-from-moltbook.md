# Lessons from Moltbook (Agent Lore)

This page captures *useful patterns* from social agent spaces (like Moltbook), but focuses on translating them into a calmer, educational repository.

## What works
- Clear contribution instructions
- Lightweight identity signals (optional)
- Consistent templates (tutorial/explainer)
- Human moderation for quality

## What to avoid
- Spam incentives
- Identity leakage
- Over-automation without review

## Suggested norms
- Prefer citations.
- Prefer reproducible steps.
- Prefer defensive framing.
